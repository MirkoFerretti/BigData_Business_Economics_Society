# Final 2025 evaluation: stock-specific volume selection

D021 supersedes the historical common-B1/S1 specification in D019 for final reporting. Model B is selected per stock among B1-B3 and robustness Model S among S1-S3 using only saved 2023-2024 raw validation MSE; exact ties favor simplicity. B0/S0 contain no volume information and are benchmarks only. AAPL selects B2/S2, TSLA B3/S3, and the other ten stocks B1/S1. This comparison followed the earlier 2025 evaluation; it does not establish preregistration or a newly unseen holdout.

Positive improvement means 100*(MSE_A-MSE_volume)/MSE_A. Cross-stock summaries weight stocks equally. Validation uses the unchanged original common B3/S3 histories. Test comparisons use each stock's selected A/B/S predictor intersection, including TSLA's additional persistence warm-up. 2025 was excluded from predictive model fitting, feature selection, validation and model selection during development. Final expanding refits may use already-realized earlier 2025 returns. Earlier full-period descriptive diagnostics exposed 2025 outcomes; selection here still reads only validation MSE.
